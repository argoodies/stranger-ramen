Privacy Policy

MiniTeller · Last updated: 5 November 2026

MiniTeller is a small single-player game for iPhone and iPad, published by ARGoodies. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information.

We do not collect any data

  • The game has no accounts of its own and no sign-in of its own.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or SDKs.
  • It makes no network requests of its own: the whole game is inside the app, and it plays offline.
  • It asks for no permissions and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game reports the achievements you unlock and your total number of wins to Game Center, so they appear in your Game Center profile and on its leaderboard. When you tap the ball that appears after a win, the game also asks Game Center for your own place on that leaderboard, to show it to you in the game. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive it. If you are not signed in, nothing is reported, and the game plays the same.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a tiny amount of data in local storage on your own device: your win count (taken up to Game Center's count if that is higher), your current and best streak, the achievements you have earned, and the language you chose. It never leaves the device (apart from the Game Center reports above), and it is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
