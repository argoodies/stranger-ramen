StoryTiny

StoryTiny is a pocket-sized game of hidden roles for one player: you and two computer players sit around a small floating table, a Storyteller runs the game, and nobody knows for sure who is who.

How to play

  • Start: walk around the table if you like, then tap the Storyteller to begin. Tap the book beside him to read the three roles.
  • Your role: each game you are dealt one of the three roles, shown in the token above your head.
  • Night: if your role acts at night, the Storyteller asks you to choose a player — tap one.
  • Day: the other two say who they are (one of them may be lying). Then you nominate a player and everyone votes; with enough hands raised, the nominee is executed.
  • The end: at the end of the game every token is shown, with the drunk and poisoned markers, so you can see what really happened.

The three roles

Each night, choose an alive player: either you or they are drunk until dusk. You can’t die.

Whoever wins, loses & whoever loses, wins, even if you are dead.

Each night, choose a player: they are poisoned. The previously poisoned player dies then becomes healthy.

A drunk or poisoned player's ability does not work. If a game reaches the seventh day, it is settled there and then: good wins.

Common questions

There is no sound. Check that your device is not muted, then tap the screen once; the music starts on the first tap.

My achievements are missing. Achievements and the wins leaderboard need you to be signed in to Game Center (Settings › Game Center). Anything earned while signed out is sent the next time you open the game signed in.

Where do I see my wins and rank? After you win a game, a small ball with the Game Center colours floats where the book was. Tap it and it tells you how many games you have won and your place on the Game Center leaderboard; tap anywhere to close it. If the rank says it is updating, Game Center has not caught up with your latest win yet.

How do I change the language? Tap the 文A button at the bottom right of the table.

How do I reset my progress? Delete and reinstall the app. Achievements already in Game Center stay there.
