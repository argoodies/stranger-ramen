Ramen Puzzle · 拉面怪奇物语

How to play

  • Tap the ground to walk around the little island under the clocktower.
  • Tap the ramen shop to go in. Tap the pot, the bowl, the soup and the toppings to make what each customer asks for, then tap the customer to serve.
  • Tap the clocktower to go in. Tap the keeper and he will take you up to see the town from the top.
  • Tap the small building on the right to visit the library.
  • Cross the bridge to the church. Tap the torch to light it and the fight begins: tap a foe to strike, and strike just as it attacks to parry. Open the chest afterwards, and use the backpack button to change what you carry.

Common questions

There is no sound. Check that your device is not muted, then tap the screen once; the music starts on the first tap.

How do I reset my progress? Delete and reinstall the app.

怎么玩

  • 点地面，在钟楼脚下的小岛上散步。
  • 点拉面店进门。点面锅、碗、汤锅和配料，按客人说的做好，再点客人把面端上。
  • 点钟楼进门。点守塔人，他会带你到塔顶看看小镇的风景。
  • 点右边的小楼，去图书馆看看。
  • 过桥去教堂。点火把把它点燃，战斗就开始了：点敌人出手，在对方攻击的瞬间出手就是弹刀。打完后打开宝箱，用背包按钮更换装备。

常见问题

没有声音。请确认设备没有静音，然后点一下屏幕；音乐会在第一次点击后开始。

怎么重置进度？删除应用后重新安装即可。
