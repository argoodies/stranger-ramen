Ramen Puzzle · 拉面怪奇物语

How to play

  • Walk: tap the ground to walk around the little island under the clocktower. The gold diamond over the ramen shop shows where to start.
  • Ramen shop: tap the shop to go in. Tap the pot to cook the noodles, then tap the bowl, the soup and the toppings to make what each customer asks for. Lift the bowl and tap the customer to serve. After every three customers, make a bowl for yourself, put it on the bar and tap it to eat.
  • Coffee house: tap whoever is behind the counter for a coffee by day or a drink at night, then tap the cup to drink it. The cat in the corner likes to be stroked.
  • Clocktower: tap the keeper and he will take you up to see the town from the top.
  • Library: the small building on the right, for a quiet moment in the light from the window.
  • Church: cross the bridge and tap the torch to light it and start the fight. Tap a foe to strike, and strike just as it attacks to parry. When the fight is won, open the chest and talk to whoever appears; then the steps out by the door appear. Lighting the torch again starts the next round. Use the backpack button to change what you carry.
  • Language: on the title screen, the globe button switches between 简体中文, 繁體中文 and English.

Common questions

There is no sound. Check that your device is not muted, then tap the screen once; the music starts on the first tap.

How do I reset my progress? Delete and reinstall the app.

Where can I get it? iPhone and iPad: the App Store. Android: the Android download. HarmonyOS NEXT: the HarmonyOS download.

拉面怪奇物语 · Ramen Puzzle

怎么玩

  • 散步：点地面，在钟楼脚下的小岛上走走。拉面店上方的金色菱形告诉你从哪里开始。
  • 拉面店：点拉面店进门。点面锅煮面，再点碗、汤锅和配料，按客人说的做好；把碗端起来，点客人把面端上。每做完三位客人，要给自己做一碗，放到吧台上，点它吃掉。
  • 咖啡馆：点柜台后面的人，白天是咖啡、晚上是酒，再点杯子喝掉。角落里的猫喜欢被摸。
  • 钟楼：点守塔人，他会带你到塔顶看看小镇的风景。
  • 图书馆：右边的小楼，在窗外的光里安静地待一会儿。
  • 教堂：过桥进教堂，点火把把它点燃，战斗就开始了。点敌人出手，在对方攻击的瞬间出手就是弹刀。打赢后打开宝箱，和出现的人说说话，门口才会出现离开的台阶；再次点燃火把就进入下一关。用背包按钮更换装备。
  • 语言：在标题画面点地球按钮，可在简体中文、繁體中文和 English 之间切换。

常见问题

没有声音。请确认设备没有静音，然后点一下屏幕；音乐会在第一次点击后开始。

怎么重置进度？删除应用后重新安装即可。

在哪里下载？iPhone 与 iPad：App Store。Android：Android 下载。HarmonyOS NEXT：鸿蒙下载。
