Privacy Policy

Ramen Puzzle · 拉面怪奇物语 · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Ramen Puzzle is a small single-player game published by ARGoodies. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

We do not collect any data

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a tiny amount of progress (for example, how many bowls of ramen you have served and which items you have found) in local storage on your own device. It never leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
