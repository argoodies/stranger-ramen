Privacy Policy

Ramen Oddities · 拉面怪奇物语 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Ramen Oddities is a small single-player game published by ARGoodies, for iPhone and iPad, Android and HarmonyOS NEXT. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information. It is the same on every platform.

We do not collect any data

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or SDKs.
  • It makes no network requests: the whole game is inside the app, and it plays offline.
  • It asks for no permissions and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a tiny amount of data in local storage on your own device: your progress (for example, how many bowls of ramen you have served and which items you have found) and the language you chose in the game. It never leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.

隐私政策

拉面怪奇物语 · Ramen Oddities · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日

《拉面怪奇物语》是 ARGoodies 发布的一款单机小游戏，支持 iPhone 与 iPad、Android 和 HarmonyOS NEXT。本政策说明游戏如何处理你的信息，各平台完全相同。

我们不收集任何数据

  • 游戏没有账号，也不需要登录。
  • 游戏不含广告、统计分析或任何第三方追踪组件与 SDK。
  • 游戏不发起任何网络请求：整个游戏都在应用里，断网也能玩。
  • 游戏不申请任何权限，不会访问你的通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风或位置。
  • 你在游戏里的任何操作都不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

保存在你设备上的内容

游戏只会把极少量的数据保存在你自己设备的本地存储里：游戏进度（例如你端出过多少碗面、找到过哪些物品），以及你在游戏里选择的语言。这些内容不会离开设备，删除应用时会一并清除。

儿童

游戏不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

变更

如果本政策有变化，新版本会连同新的日期发布在本页面。

联系

对本政策有疑问，请见支持页面。
